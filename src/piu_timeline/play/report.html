Note timing comparison

Times are absolute recording positions. Input − target includes uncertainty in both ranges: negative is early, positive is late. Overlapping ranges do not establish an early/late result. Unmatched notes show alternative observed refresh ranges or conditional bounds from neighboring processed rows; these do not establish an input assignment. When evidence is absent, the input stays unconfirmed; a possible MISS never supplies one. Chord heads share a refresh bracket; aggregate brackets can span several rows.
